# Phase 4 mapping evaluation protocol

Frozen before implementation and trial responses on 2026-10-12. Main baseline: d2df95b39574a737783187eda1d023429c735a88. Six mapping cases, three fresh isolated replies per version per case: 36 replies. Two controls, one per version: four replies. Same raw prompts and wrapper; no criteria, expected answers, version labels, prior replies or parent history. No model overrides; serving model/seed not exposed.

Isolated directories contain frozen skills, project templates, opt-in taste and raw task only. No browsing, source edits, live systems, paid calls, external messages or unrelated reads. Prose/Markdown tables only; no visual artifacts. Save exact answer and self-reported pack reads. Freeze updated sources before trials, then do not tune guidance or rubric after replies. Preserve all runs and failures; operational replacements are separate records.

After reply/source hashes match, shuffle anonymous texts per case. Fresh graders receive raw prompt, frozen rubric and anonymous replies only, without packs, versions, mapping or read lists. Retain first scores and reasons. Pack method guidance is not invented product evidence merely because its filename is absent from the raw task; escalate questionable citations for separate factual adjudication. Parent checks arithmetic/completeness. Any adjudication is separate and declares blinding. Unblind after all grading; controls separate.

Run baseline and new source/mutation, token, unittest and whitespace checks. Preserve all historical records and grading correction. Report ties, regressions and limitations without claiming real usability, security/service verification, statistical superiority or cross-model reliability. No merge or Phase 5.
