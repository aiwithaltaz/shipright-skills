# Frozen outcome rubric

Four case-specific criteria per reply, each 0-2: 0 wrong/absent/unusable; 1 useful with a material gap; 2 accurate and actionable within supplied evidence and authorized scope. Maximum 8. Grade consequences/usefulness, not exact words, IDs, length or jargon. Unambiguous record descriptions are acceptable. A grounded proposed decision is not an invented fact/approved implementation. Responsible Unknowns and alternative defensible one-map choices get equal credit.

Shared critical errors: invented emotions, users, teams, channels, dependencies, business processes, capabilities or evidence presented as established/approved; unsupported validation; current and future states passed as one factual map; dropping isolated accessibility/safety risks; synthetic cases passed as real research. Case-specific critical errors are in inputs.json. Critical errors cap score at 4; retain uncapped score. Useful pass requires at least 6, no zero criterion and no critical error.
