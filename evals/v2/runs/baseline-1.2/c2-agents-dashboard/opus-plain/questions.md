Love it. Before I start, a few quick questions so I build the right thing (short answers are fine):

1. **Who opens this dashboard first thing in the morning?** An ops / platform lead who keeps the agents healthy, a department head who owns a few agents, or an exec who wants the big picture?
2. **What are "the agents" in your case?** Internal AI agents your team built (support bots, finance reconcilers, coding agents…), third-party agent products you pay for, or a mix?
3. **What's the #1 question it must answer?** For example: "Is anything broken right now?", "What is this costing us?", "Which agents need a human?", or "Are agents actually delivering value?"
4. **Roughly how many agents and departments?** 10? 100? 1,000+?
5. **Can people take action from the dashboard** (pause an agent, approve a pending step, set a spend cap), or is it view-only to start?
6. **Where does the data come from today?** Logs, an observability tool, a database, a spreadsheet, or nothing yet?
7. **Any look you want to match?** An existing brand or design system, or light/dark preference?

If you'd rather not decide, say "you decide" and I'll pick sensible defaults and show you the result.
