Happy to build it! A few quick questions so I build the right thing:

1. **What's the website for?** A business, a portfolio, a personal project, an event, something else? A sentence about what you do helps a lot.
2. **What should a visitor do on it?** For example: find your hours and address, see what you offer, book or order something, contact you, sign up for something.
3. **What details should be on it?** Name, location, hours, services or products, prices, contact info, anything a customer always asks about.
4. **Any look or feel in mind?** Colors, a logo, sites you like, or words like "cozy", "minimal", "bold".
5. **One page or several?** If you're not sure, I'll start with a single page that covers everything and we can grow it later.

If you'd rather not decide some of this, just tell me what it's for and I'll make sensible calls on the rest.
