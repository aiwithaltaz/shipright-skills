About us

Flour, water, salt, and a lot of patience.

Crumb & Co is a small bakery on East 6th Street. Every loaf starts with our own sourdough starter and rests for a day and a half before it meets the oven. Our pastries take three days. It's slow on purpose: time is what gives good bread its flavor.

We bake early, sell out often, and like knowing our regulars by their usual order. Come say hi.
