Love it, let's build something. "SaaS" covers a lot of ground though, so before I start planning, a few quick questions:

1. **Who is it for?** What kind of business or person would pay for this? (e.g. dentists, gyms, freelancers, landscaping companies, agencies...)
2. **What problem does it solve?** What's the one annoying job they do today (in spreadsheets, paper, texts, or a clunky tool) that your software should make easier?
3. **How big are your customers?** Solo operators, small teams (under 20 people), or larger companies?
4. **What do you already have?** Just the idea, some customer conversations, a design, or existing code?
5. **What do you want from me right now?** A full plan (scope, screens, data, pricing, launch steps), a clickable prototype of the main screen, or both?

If you're not sure about some of these, just tell me who it's for and what problem it solves, and I'll make sensible calls on the rest.
