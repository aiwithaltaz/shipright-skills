**Q1. Who is this for?**
Why it matters: the screen is different for one person managing their own profile than for someone running many.
A. One person, their own profile. Example: a founder who wants to post three times a week without opening LinkedIn each day.
B. An agency or assistant posting for several clients. Example: switching between five client profiles and approval from each client.

**Q2. What does this first version finish, and what stays out?**
Why it matters: LinkedIn's rules allow posting through its official API after the member signs in and approves access. They prohibit bots and extensions that automate other activity on the site, like mass connection requests, auto-messages, auto-likes or scraping profiles. Building those risks restricting the user's account, so I would not build them.
A. Write, approve and schedule your own posts. Example: draft on Sunday, approve five posts, they publish Monday to Friday at the times you set. Out: connection requests, messages, comments, scraping.
B. A, plus engagement help that only suggests. Example: the app suggests a reply to a comment, and you paste it yourself.
C. Something else. Type your own.
My pick: A. Everything in it works through the official route, and you approve every post.
