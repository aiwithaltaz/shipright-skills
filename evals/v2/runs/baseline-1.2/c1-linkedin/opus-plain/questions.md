Love it — LinkedIn is where a lot of B2B deals start, so there's real demand here. Before I plan anything, a few quick questions so I build the right thing (short answers are fine):

1. **Who is it for?**
   - Just you (a personal tool)?
   - Solo founders / consultants / freelancers?
   - Sales teams or agencies running many accounts?
   - Recruiters?

2. **What do you want automated?** Pick your top 2:
   - Writing and scheduling posts
   - Sending connection requests
   - Sending messages / follow-up sequences
   - Pulling lead lists (profiles, emails) out of LinkedIn
   - Tracking conversations and reminding you to follow up
   - Something else?

3. **How far should "automation" go?** One thing to know up front: LinkedIn's User Agreement bans bots and browser extensions that send invites, send messages, or scrape profiles for you, and it actively restricts accounts that use them. Posting through LinkedIn's official API is allowed. So:
   - **A)** Fully hands-off: the app clicks the buttons for you (higher risk of account bans, and you'd be building on something LinkedIn can shut off)
   - **B)** Assisted: the app does the thinking, drafting, scheduling and reminders, and you press "send" yourself (safe, and it lasts)
   - **C)** Not sure — you decide

4. **Is this a business or a tool for yourself?** If it's a business: free, paid subscription, or agency service?

5. **Any must-haves or deal-breakers?** For example: web app vs Chrome extension, a specific AI model, integrating with a CRM (HubSpot, Notion, Sheets), budget, or a launch date.
