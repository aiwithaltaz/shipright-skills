A live tool that makes faceless YouTube videos

SocialGhost is not a mockup. It is live now, and a limited group of beta testers gets in first.

Testers use it to make faceless videos for YouTube. Then they tell us what worked, what failed and what was missing.

How the beta works

Four steps. The last one decides whether access stays free.

  1. Apply

    Answer four short questions below. Tell us about your channel and what you want to make.

  2. We pick testers

    The beta is limited, so not every applicant gets in. We email the people we choose.

  3. Make videos

    Use SocialGhost to make faceless YouTube videos the way you would for your own channel.

  4. Send feedback

    Within 2 to 3 days, send useful feedback. Free access depends on it.

What useful feedback includes

  • What you tried to make
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Where you got stuck or gave up
  • What you would change first

Apply if you will use it and report back

Apply if

  • You make faceless YouTube videos, or plan to start a faceless channel.
  • You can try SocialGhost and write feedback within 2 to 3 days.
  • You will tell us what did not work, not only what did.

Skip this beta if

  • You only want to look around.
  • You cannot send feedback within 2 to 3 days.
  • You expect a finished product with no rough edges.

Questions before you apply

Is SocialGhost really live?

Yes. It is a live tool. The beta is limited, so access is by application.

What does free access cost?

Nothing for beta testers who send useful feedback within 2 to 3 days.

What if I cannot send feedback in time?

Free access depends on useful feedback in that window. If you cannot do it, please do not apply.

What happens after the beta?

Pricing after the beta is not published on this page.

How many testers will you accept?

The beta is limited. The number of spots is not listed.

What does the form ask?

Your email, your YouTube channel or the kind of channel you want to start, what you want to make, and a yes to the feedback window.
