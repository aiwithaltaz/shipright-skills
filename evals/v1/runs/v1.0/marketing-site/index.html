Who we are looking for

The beta works when testers make real videos and tell us what happened.

A good fit

  • You run a faceless YouTube channel, or you are about to start one.
  • You can make at least one real video in your first 2 to 3 days.
  • You will tell us plainly what worked, what broke and what was slow.

Not a fit yet

  • You want to look around but have no time this week.
  • You do not plan to publish on YouTube.
  • You would rather not send feedback.

What useful feedback looks like

Short and specific beats long and polite. Good feedback answers these four questions.

  1. What video were you trying to make?
  2. Where did you get stuck or slow down?
  3. Would you post the result as it is? Why or why not?
  4. What would make you use it every week?
