Before I write the build pack, three quick choices. Answer with letters, or say "you decide" for any of them.

**Q1. Who uses it, and what is the one job this first version finishes?**
Why it matters: it decides the main screen, who signs in and what each person can change.
A. The agency's own team, led by the owner or project leads. Job: see which client projects have a late or close deadline or are using up their budget, then open one and update it. Example: on Monday the owner sees "Brand refresh, 2 days late" at the top of the list.
B. Your clients too. Each client signs in and sees only their own projects. Example: a client checks the next deadline on their website project.
C. Something else. Tell me who.
My pick: A. Clients signing in adds accounts, permissions and polish that a first version does not need.
You decide: delegate it. Let me decide: keep it open.

**Q2. What stays out of this first version?**
Why it matters: it decides how "budget used" is tracked, and how big the build is.
A. Leave out time tracking, invoicing, payments, client logins, reports and charts, email reminders and file uploads. The project lead types in the amount spent so far. Example: "Spent so far: 12,400 of 18,000".
B. Include time tracking. Spend is worked out from hours logged times each person's rate. Example: Alex logs 3 hours on Tuesday.
C. Something else. Tell me what must be in.
My pick: A. It is the smallest version that answers "are we late or over budget?". Time tracking can come next if typing the spend gets tedious.
You decide: delegate it. Let me decide: keep it open.

B. A calm, dense work-tool look I recommend: light gray background, white table, one blue accent for actions, clear status labels. You can swap in your agency's colors later.
C. Something else. Describe it or share a reference.
My pick: B, unless you already have a brand or design system.
