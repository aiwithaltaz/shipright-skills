How the beta works

Free access, in return for useful feedback within 2 to 3 days of getting access.

  1. Apply

    Answer six short questions at the end of this page.

  2. Get picked

    The beta is limited. We email the testers we pick.

  3. Make a real video

    Use SocialGhost free on a video you actually want to make.

  4. Send feedback

    Tell us how it went within 2 to 3 days of getting access.

What useful feedback covers

Short and honest beats long and polite.

  • What you tried to make, and for which channel.
  • Where you got stuck or confused, and what you did next.
  • What the finished video got right and what it got wrong.
  • Whether you would post it, and why or why not.

Who should apply

The beta needs people who will actually use it this week.

A good fit if you

  • Run a faceless YouTube channel, or are ready to start one.
  • Have a real video idea you want to make this week.
  • Will make at least one video with SocialGhost.
  • Can send honest feedback within 2 to 3 days of getting access.

Not a fit if you

  • Only want a quick look around.
  • Cannot make time in the next few days.
  • Would rather wait until the beta is over.

Questions

Does it cost anything?
No. Beta testers get free access. In return, we ask for useful feedback within 2 to 3 days of getting access.
Do I have to be on camera?
No. SocialGhost makes faceless videos, so you never appear on screen.
Is SocialGhost ready to use?
Yes. It is live now. The beta is limited, so not everyone who applies gets a spot.
Do I need a YouTube channel already?
No. New creators can apply. The form asks whether you have a channel.
What happens after I apply?
We review applications and email the testers we pick, at the address you give us.
