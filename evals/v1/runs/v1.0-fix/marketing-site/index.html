Who should apply

This beta is for people who will actually make videos with it this week.

Apply if

  • You run a faceless YouTube channel, or you are about to start one.
  • You can try SocialGhost on a real video idea in your first 2 to 3 days.
  • You will tell us plainly what worked and what did not.

Maybe not yet if

  • You want to be on camera yourself. SocialGhost makes faceless videos.
  • You cannot set aside time in the next few days.
  • You want a finished product with no rough edges. This is a beta.

    Use SocialGhost on ideas you would actually post.

What useful feedback looks like

Answer these four questions about each video you try.

  1. What did you try to make?The video idea and who it was for.
  2. What happened?Where it worked, where it went wrong, where you got stuck.
  3. What did you expect instead?What you would have needed to post it.
  4. Would you publish it?Yes or no, and the main reason.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Beta testers get free access in return for useful feedback within 2 to 3 days of getting access.

What counts as useful feedback?

Specific answers to the four questions above, based on videos you actually tried to make. "It's great" does not help us. "I would not publish this, because..." does.

Is SocialGhost finished?

No. It is live and you can use it today, but it is in beta. You will find rough edges. Finding them is the job.

Do I need to show my face?

No. SocialGhost makes faceless videos, so you never appear on camera.

What happens after I apply?

We review applications and email the people we pick. Your 2 to 3 days start when you get access, not when you apply.
