**Q1. Who should the site invite to apply?**
Why it matters: it sets the headline, the "who should apply" section and the questions on the form.
- A. Anyone curious about faceless YouTube. Example: "Try SocialGhost free."

My pick: B. "Serious" testers are the goal, so the page should filter as much as it invites.
