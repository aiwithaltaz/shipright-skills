**What I decided for you (Proposed)**
- Visitors turn the speaker in 3D, then click Buy to go to your existing store. No checkout on this site.
- Release 1 is one home page plus a not-found page. No cart, accounts, reviews, configurator, FAQ or newsletter.
- A dark showroom look with one warm metal accent and the Manrope font.
- Astro with plain three.js, on a static host.
- One signature move: the speaker turns once when the 3D view loads. With reduced motion on, people see still views and can open 3D themselves.
- Until you send a real model, the speaker is a placeholder built in code. "Model 01" is a working name.
