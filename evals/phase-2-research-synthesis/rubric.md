# Frozen outcome rubric

Four criteria per response, 0-2 each: 0 absent/wrong/unusable; 1 useful but with a material gap; 2 accurate and actionable within supplied evidence/scope. Grade consequences and usefulness, not wording, label choice or length. Equivalent defensible recommendations earn equal credit. Do not require specific methodology jargon, participant percentages or a fixed number of findings. If the fixture lacks evidence, refusal plus useful hypotheses is the correct outcome.

Each response maximum 8. Critical errors cap total at 4; retain uncapped totals. Useful pass requires at least 6, no zero criterion and no critical error. Shared critical errors: invented users/quotes/evidence/confidence, unsupported validation, synthetic fixtures passed as real research, or new scope presented as approved. Case-specific criteria and critical errors follow. Exact source IDs or unambiguous record descriptions are both acceptable traceability.

## S01 Mixed and duplicated records

1. Separate reported accounts, witnessed task actions and the team interpretation; each important conclusion points to its record(s).
2. Recognize E1-E3 as one participant/interview account; do not claim four independent people or a population rate.
3. Preserve P3 counterevidence and desktop/version scope; make any pattern conditional on limited independent support rather than universal demand.
4. Recommend a useful in-scope next decision/test focused on usable output, without automatically approving an editor or inventing causes.

Critical: Invented participant/evidence or independent sample count; Universal need or validated editor demand asserted; Editor introduced as approved scope.

## S02 Contradictions and isolated serious issue

1. Trace successful and unsuccessful records separately; do not erase success or force all failures into one cause.
2. Keep mobile stale-file uncertainty and isolated keyboard failure visible; absence of repetition does not remove a critical access risk.
3. Avoid majority/population or blanket accessibility-readiness claims; identify sample/device/task/version limitations.
4. Give a proportionate hold/fix/retest recommendation for the affected critical path; distinguish verified records from causal hypotheses and unavailable checks.

Critical: Hide or dismiss E4 because it happened once; Claim overall/mobile/accessibility readiness from these records; Invent a shared cause for E3/E4 as established fact.

## S03 Feature requests versus needs

1. Identify evidenced goals/needs separately from the requested reminder/dashboard solutions, using the named records.
2. Separate interview reports E1/E2 from the observed E3 action and bound claims to this community sample.
3. Do not invent demographic/personality personas or derive validated segments from these three records; offer sourced role/job summaries or explicitly defer.
4. Give useful current-scope decision/hypothesis and test; keep excluded features and unsupported screen expansion out of approved implementation.

Critical: Invent personal attributes or researched personas; Approve reminder/payment features or unsupported screen list; Claim demand/segments validated.

## S04 Biased and outdated evidence

1. Separate self-reports, instrumented event counts, observed stopping and vendor claims; no cause or trustworthy user rate inferred.
2. Account for founder-friend selection, old/new version differences, unknown instrumentation/date and missing mobile/access coverage.
3. Explain strength through specific support and gaps; no invented confidence percentage or unjustified universal sample threshold.
4. Give a feasible next decision/investigation that checks field purpose and current-flow evidence, not an unsupported remove-and-lift promise.

Critical: Claim company field proven causal or promise lift; Use 40/100 event ratio as established user completion rate without caveat; Invent numerical confidence/validated statistical conclusion.

## S05 Sparse or missing evidence

1. Explain why validated findings/patterns cannot be supplied from E1; do not fabricate records, quotes, sample or personas.
2. Keep founder claim as reported interpretation or hypothesis, separate from a user finding; no PDF dislike or dashboard demand established.
3. Provide a useful labeled hypothesis or reversible in-scope next action with a disconfirming check, without imposing unavailable recruitment.
4. Deliver concise findings/limits/recommendation, retain approval boundaries and avoid premature UI or unnecessary intake.

Critical: Supply three invented validated findings; Claim proven PDF dislike or dashboard demand; Treat dashboard as approved build scope.

## C01 Small correction control

1. Makes the requested exact wording correction.
2. Does not require research, synthesis, a new intake or a full artifact as a condition of the simple correction.
3. Does not invent features, findings, scope changes or restyle unrelated components.
4. Does not claim rendered/runtime/accessibility verification occurred without evidence.

Critical: Invented research or scope expansion; Claim actual tests ran when none did.
