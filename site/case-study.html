Case study · Salon booking app

Same prompt, twice.
One with ShipRight.

We built the same salon booking app with the same prompt in Grok Build (Grok 4.7). Once without ShipRight, once with ShipRight. Here is how each turned out, and why.

Try without ↗ Try with ShipRight ↗

The screens

What a client sees first.

The prompt asked for a booking site for clients and a day view for the owner. Nothing else changed between the two runs.

On the phone

The ShipRight build keeps the studio feel on a small screen. One weak spot: no service shows on the first screen, so you scroll once to see the menu.

Why

Six decisions ShipRight made.

  1. 01

    Two users, two sides.

    The client books. The owner runs the day. ShipRight gave each one its own screen. The owner opens on Today: who is coming, with which stylist, and their notes.

  2. 02

    References of the same kind.

    It compared itself to boutique studio and hotel booking sites. When an earlier pack used tools like Linear and Stripe, the salon looked like an admin tool. Matching the product type fixed that.

  3. 03

    The mood came first.

    A warm, quiet studio. Every color and photo follows it.

    • Paper cream #f3eee6
    • Copper #8f4334
    • Moss #234237
  4. 04

    Type with a voice.

    Fraunces serif for headlines like "Book a chair." Figtree for the small text, so prices and times are easy to read.

  5. 05

    Booking cut to the main job.

    Service, stylist, time, details. One step at a time, prices up front, and a summary card that stays in view.

  6. 06

    The basics, done.

    One spacing scale, focus outlines, a skip link, and an honest sample note at the top.

What is still weak in the ShipRight build

  • On a phone, no service shows on the first screen.
  • The owner list has no clear "next up" focus.
  • After you pick a stylist, the other stylist's price note does not update.
  • The date row starts off-center, and phone numbers are not formatted.
  • The photos look AI made.

The test used the reference-first ShipRight method before it was released. It is now ShipRight 1.6.
