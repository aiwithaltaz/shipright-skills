How it works

Nine steps, in order.

The thinking comes before any pixels. Each step writes a short note the next one uses. The pictures come from the salon test; the notes are shortened examples.

Part 1

Think about the product

  1. 01

    A basic idea

    You type one line. ShipRight turns it into a short paragraph: what it is, who pays, and the job it does every day.

    idea.mdIllustrative

    • A booking site for a small hair salon
    • Clients book a visit, the owner runs the day
    • The job: fill the chairs without phone calls
  2. 02

    Research

    It looks at the best products of the same type, what they do well, and where people get stuck.

    research.mdIllustrative

    • Boutique studio and hotel booking sites
    • Prices shown up front win trust
    • Pain: long forms before you see a time
  3. 03

    Define the users

    A main user and a second one. Their day, why they open the app, and what worries them.

    users.mdIllustrative

    • Client: books in a short break, wants a clear price
    • Owner: needs to see who is next, fast
    • Two users, so two sides
  4. 04

    The PRD

    What each user sees first, the few jobs that matter, and what stays out of version one.

    prd.mdIllustrative

    • Client first screen: the offer and a Book button
    • Owner first screen: today's visits
    • Out of v1: payments, reviews
  5. 05

    Flows and states

    The 3 to 5 main jobs in a few clicks each, plus empty, loading and error states, and real looking sample data.

    flows.mdIllustrative

    • Book: service, stylist, time, details
    • Owner: mark done, add a walk-in
    • Empty day, failed save, long names

Part 2

Design from real references, then build

  1. 06

    References and brand mood

    It names the product type, picks 2 or 3 great references of that type, and sets a mood in three words.

  2. 07

    Design system

    Two fonts, a short type scale, one spacing scale and about ten colors, all in one file.

    Type steps

  3. 08

    Build the UI

    Every screen is built to sit next to its references, with short, real copy.

  4. 09

    Checks

    Screenshots at desktop and phone size, compared with the references. It fixes the weakest parts, then checks for broken things.
