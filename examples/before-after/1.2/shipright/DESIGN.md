# Proposed design system
Source: delegated creative decisions; directions remain Proposed until owner choice. Same product, distinct story devices in docs/shipright/directions.md.

Two scales: display headings and compact reading/control type. Structural whitespace supplies hierarchy; use one accent, no gradient text. Tokens in design/tokens.css are provisional direction A values and change with owner selection. Shared spacing 8/16/24/40/64/96px; control radius 2px; paper elevation restrained; native focus outline 3px with offset. Body at least 16px; controls at least 44px tall. No fake chart, logo strip, rating or decorative sphere.

Components: visible site navigation, native anchor, install copy button with adjacent status, selectable code line, truthful product document, five-mode index, source citation. Links underline or clearly expose affordance; primary action consistently install. Mobile puts heading before hero and unfolds pinned content into natural reading order. Motion is optional; reduced motion preserves every word and product fragment. Selection of D3 fixes final palette/type/technique choices.
