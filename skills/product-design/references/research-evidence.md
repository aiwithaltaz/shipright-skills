# Research evidence

## Purpose and applicability

Prevent unsupported product commitments. Use for consequential claims, supplied research or provisional direction; skip evidence paperwork for a clear copy/spacing fix.
Separating evidence from interpretation prevents approval being mistaken for validation.

## Record

Use existing notes, not a mandatory document:

| ID/claim | Kind/basis | Source/date/version | Sample/context | Limitation | Decision/test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- **Observed:** directly inspected action/artifact; identify the record.
- **Reported:** participant/stakeholder/vendor statement; not an observed action.
- **Inferred:** interpretation with supporting evidence IDs and alternatives.
- **Hypothesis:** testable proposal plus disconfirming result; facts remain Unknown.
- **Unknown:** unavailable or insufficient evidence; name what would resolve it.

These are evidence kinds, not gate statuses. Facts have provenance; approval controls scope, not truth.
Confidence needs a basis, never invented percentages.
Keep contradictory records visible; grouping/synthesis belongs to Phase 2.

## Edges and delivery

Missing dates, samples, event definitions, outdated versions or inaccessible sources limit claims.
Interviews about behavior are reports unless the behavior was actually observed.
Analytics describe instrumented behavior, not its cause; inspect denominator, timeframe and event quality.
Competitor claims and attractive screenshots establish neither usability gains nor demand.
Simulated participants and example quotes are not research.

Continue reversible work with labeled hypotheses; reserve unknown permissions/destructive effects.
Accessibility/device gaps remain gaps, not evidence of inclusive usability.
Responsive UI is not specified here; record tested devices and contexts.
Builders receive approved requirements plus relevant evidence limits, never raw personal data.
Verify every claim traces to a supplied/inspected source and its stated limits.
