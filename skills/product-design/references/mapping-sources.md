# Mapping sources and limits

Original summaries below guide method selection; they are not this product's findings or business processes. Public page text inspected 2026-10-11. No paid courses, copied templates, diagrams or source illustrations are included. Keep source publication/update dates separate from access dates. A method's review date is not evidence a product was tested.

| Source | Publication / update | Original takeaway and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NN/g, [Journey Mapping 101](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/) | 2018-12-09; reviewed 2026-07-15 | Scope an actor's goal/scenario and experience over time. Actions and sourced feelings can clarify opportunities; a framework never supplies missing emotion, persona or evidence. |
| NN/g, [Service Blueprints: Definition](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/service-blueprints-definition/) | 2017-08-27; reviewed 2026-07-15 | Connect customer actions with visible interactions, invisible delivery and supporting work. Visibility defines lanes; service props called evidence must not be confused with research support. |
| NN/g, [UX Mapping Methods Compared](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-mapping-cheat-sheet/) | 2017-11-05 | Select the representation for the decision and perspective. Mapping methods answer different questions; producing every map is not required. |
| NN/g, [Research for Journey Mapping](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/research-journey-mapping/) | 2019-02-10 | Inspect relevant existing records and choose research for missing decisions. Assumptions need verification; an expert-authored map is not performed research. |
| NN/g, [Five Steps to Service Blueprinting](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/5-steps-service-blueprinting/) | 2018-02-04 | Bound the scenario and distinguish current delivery from proposed improvements. Backstage claims need staff/process evidence; a workshop is optional, not a compulsory gate. |
| NN/g, [Task Analysis](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/task-analysis/) | 2020-09-20 | Understand a goal through actual actions, conditions and branches. A user's task is distinct from the organization's delivery process; no generic sequence proves actual behavior. |

## User collection

Relevant mapping sections of the UXpert AI guide and MASTERBLUEPRINT Contextual UX/UI Agent System Knowledge Base were inspected through the user's collection on 2026-10-11. These compiled secondary guides contain illustrations and some incomplete placeholders. UXpert AI's sample stages, feelings, quotes and channels are authored examples, not actual participant evidence. The knowledge base distinguishes longer-term journey research from staff/process research for blueprints. The exact NN/g mapping links were verified on the public pages separately; do not claim the collection contains them. Private source contents and identifiers are not republished.

Do not copy a sample journey, emotional curve or blueprint into a product as a finding. Use collection resources as guidance only. Unknown authorship/context and incomplete passages limit authority; private guide access does not establish any product's evidence, permission or system capability.
