# Evidence-grounded user journeys

Use for one sourced actor's goal and experience across relevant time/stages or channels, when task steps alone miss a decision. Establish actor, scenario, start/end, phase and current or proposed state. Use actual records, not a newly invented persona. Do not force awareness, purchase and loyalty stages onto unrelated tasks.

## Build only the supported journey

Record stages/actions in the sequence established by evidence. A useful compact format is stage/action, channel, evidence kind/source, pain or positive outcome, unanswered question. Add entry, exit, interruption, return and recovery where known or consequential. Omit unnecessary lanes; a partial journey is valid.

Keep different people's accounts separate; do not splice them into one observed path. A synthesis across records can identify a bounded pattern only when its evidence supports that connection. Retain contrary and successful accounts with their context. Repeated notes from the same event are not independent participants. Unknown samples, later outcomes and causes remain Unknown; a journey does not supply confidence.

Keep recalled behavior and feelings Reported, even in a recorded interview. Directly witnessed actions are Observed. An observed pause does not establish frustration. An abandoned page does not prove rejection, cancellation or failed payment. Use supplied emotion words only with their source and kind; do not invent emotions or an emotional curve. Missing emotions can be Unknown or omitted. Do not infer feelings from facial expression, stage names or expert intuition.

## Risk and recovery

Retain isolated accessibility and safety issues even outside repeated patterns. Keep what happened, affected goal, kind/source and limits visible in the risk summary. Uncertain severity or cause does not justify omission. Distinguish observed independent success from reported success, prompted verification and assisted completion using existing research guidance.

For interruption/return, identify known saved work, remaining action and state; missing persistence stays Unknown. Leaving an experience is not proof a submitted action was cancelled. Use states-and-flows.md for effects and canonical F1-F3 rather than redefining them. Use operational-flows.md for actual prerequisites or human pending work. Keep channels grounded in supplied evidence; do not add reminders, email, phone or support to make the journey complete.

## Turn the map into a decision

Separate current evidence from Proposed future changes in a distinct section or column. Future stages/actions must follow authorized goals and be labeled as recommendations; do not assume new capabilities or processes. Each material recommendation names its source or hypothesis, uncertainty, approval needed and next verification. A supplied complaint is not automatic approval for its requested feature.

Show concise findings, risks and recommended decisions. Include the text map only when useful/requested. Detailed records stay behind the summary and private identities do not enter builder exports. Missing evidence permits a labeled hypothesis and a proportionate optional research plan; no invented participants, sessions or validation. Do not jump directly to UI.
