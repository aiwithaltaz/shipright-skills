# Research to product decisions

## Purpose and applicability

Use when findings or evidence gaps could change a product commitment. Skip pure summaries and clear corrections. Work from sourced records/findings or explicitly labeled hypotheses. Interpretation supports a recommendation; it does not approve scope or validate a solution.

## Decide before UI

1. Name the current decision, approved phase and constraints. Connect it to finding/record IDs, affected jobs and desired outcomes.
2. Separate the evidenced need from requested features. Identify material alternatives, including preserving the current approach, correcting a known defect or gathering missing evidence. Do not fill an option quota.
3. Weigh consequence if wrong, reversibility, evidence limits, contradictory findings and access risks. Frequency alone does not determine priority. Never manufacture impact, effort, revenue or confidence numbers.
4. Recommend the smallest justified commitment. A proposed opportunity is not an approved feature. Keep new features/roles, navigation and screen lists out of implementation unless already authorized; authorized IA organization uses product-design guidance; new scope still requires authority, and visual design belongs to its next authorized stage.
5. Specify what would challenge the recommendation and the smallest feasible verification. Follow Phase 1 method/planning guidance only when a new study is actually needed. No recruitment, payments, messages or instrumentation installation follows automatically.

## Decision record and output

| Decision / status | Need / finding IDs | Rationale and alternatives | Risk / unresolved evidence | Test / stopping condition | Owner / approval source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Reuse shared Proposed/Approved/Unresolved/Superseded statuses. Keep evidence confidence separate from approval and gate statuses. A strong finding can justify a proposed action without establishing the chosen solution will work. Weak evidence supports a reversible hypothesis or a deferred affected commitment, not validated requirements. Unknown access/destructive effects remain unresolved under the shared contract.

For the user, lead with the recommendation, concise findings and material risks. Avoid raw transcript dumps, mandatory personas, a methodology menu or lengthy internal reasoning. Preserve the evidence trail in existing working notes; write requested documents only. A changed finding must update affected proposed decisions and verification needs without rewriting older evidence.

## Edges and verification

Incomplete, biased, stale or contradictory samples limit commitments. A single serious accessibility failure may require a targeted fix/retest despite successful sessions; do not claim overall accessibility readiness. Include affected devices, access needs and responsive verification limits. This file specifies decision boundaries, not new layouts.

Builders receive current approved scope, sourced needs, unresolved proposals and acceptance/verification conditions. Verify every recommended commitment traces to a need/evidence or a clearly labeled hypothesis, respects authority and has an actionable stopping point. No finding-to-UI shortcut, invented persona or validation claim.
