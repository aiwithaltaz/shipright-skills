# Research method selection

## Purpose and triage

Choose the smallest way to resolve a decision, not a research ritual.
Inspect existing evidence. Skip new research for sufficient current evidence or bounded corrections.
Otherwise name the uncertain decision, impact if wrong, available resources and stopping point.
Unavailable research permits labeled hypotheses, not validated conclusions.

## Choose

| Method/source | Use / why | Reject or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public/source review | Check published constraints and alternatives cheaply | Cannot establish this product's user behavior/demand |
| Interview | Learn reported context, needs and experience | Leading/future-intent answers do not prove behavior |
| Context observation | Understand actual work and dependencies | Access, privacy and observer effects constrain it |
| Usability tasks | Observe whether a target user completes a flow | Requires a usable artifact; does not prove market demand |
| Analytics | Locate measured activity at scale | Missing events/denominators/version cannot establish reliable rates or causes |
| Survey | Measure reported views with suitable sampling | Small/self-selected samples cannot represent a population |

Attitudinal means reported beliefs/experience; behavioral means observed/measured actions.
Classify each record: hybrid sessions can collect both.
Qualitative explores how/why; quantitative estimates how many/how much with adequate sampling.
A/B testing needs traffic, sound instrumentation and a statistical plan; do not recommend automatically.
Card sorting/tree testing detail belongs to Phase 3.

## Shared contract

Edges: unavailable participants/tools, contradictory/stale sources and insufficient samples.
Accessibility: plan relevant access needs and usable materials; expert review is not participant testing.
Responsive: study relevant devices/contexts; do not generalize desktop evidence to mobile.
Implementation: carry uncertainties and acceptance checks forward; add no feature or analytics SDK automatically.
Verify method answers the named question, limits are stated, and the stopping point is met.
Original sources: NN1, NN2 and G1-G3 in the source register linked from SKILL.md.
