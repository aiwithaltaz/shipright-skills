# Lightweight research planning

## Purpose and applicability

Use for consequential questions/guides; skip clear corrections or sufficient evidence. Plans are unperformed.

## Plan

State decision/question, evidence, hypothesis, what would challenge it, method, participants, devices/access, scope/timebox, owner and stopping point.
Choose samples for method/diversity; no universal five-user rule or statistical proof.
Without recruitment/tools, mark the plan unperformed; continue supported work.

Optional framing aids:
- **Opportunity Solution Tree:** link owned outcomes, evidenced opportunities and risky assumptions; not validation (P1).
- **Value Proposition Canvas:** frame proposed jobs/pains/gains and offer-fit hypotheses; not personas or validated demand (S1).

No Phase 2 synthesis or new features.

## Guides and safeguards

Interview: approved consent, neutral recent-task prompts, follow-ups, close; no selling.
Every interview guide states: recalled behavior is Reported; behavior directly witnessed during the session is Observed. Narration alone is not observation.
Usability: relevant goal without naming the control/path; observe actions, record assistance and prompt timing.
Record independent completion before verification prompts; prompted recovery is assisted.
Match instructions and outcome tables:

| Category | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Independent completion | Success directly observed before prompts/help. |
| Prompted verification | Non-directional check confirms prior success; independence unverified. |
| Assisted completion | Directional help or prompted recovery enables success. |
| Setup or technical failure | Assessment blocked; separate cause from product-task failure. |

Unobserved steps remain unverified; record incomplete tasks, withdrawals and timeouts.
Use safe test data; label simulation limits.
Consent, accessible materials, voluntary exit, minimal/secure data and approved retention precede sessions.
Use customary devices/assistive technology; cover mobile, connection/environment constraints.

## Handoff and verification

A guide does not authorize outreach, recording, payment or personal-data collection.
Do not invent consent/legal text or privacy policy.
Handoff scope/limitations/tests, never participant identities.
No responsive UI contract is generated; specify study devices.
Verify neutral prompts, evidence distinctions, consistent outcomes, feasible access, prerequisites and stopping point.
