# Lightweight research planning

## Purpose and applicability

Use for consequential study questions or requested guides.
Skip studies for clear corrections or sufficient evidence.
A decision-linked plan makes observations usable without pretending research has happened.

## Plan

State: decision/question, existing evidence, hypothesis, what would challenge it, method, participant criteria, device/access needs, scope/timebox, owner and stopping point.
Choose sample/round size for the method and diversity; no universal five-user rule or claim of statistical validation.
If recruitment/tools are unavailable, mark the plan unperformed and continue supported work.

Optional framing aids:
- **Opportunity Solution Tree:** connect an owned outcome to evidenced opportunities and risky assumptions. Reject invented opportunities; a completed tree is not validation (P1).
- **Value Proposition Canvas:** identify proposed jobs, pains, gains and offer-fit assumptions. Reject assumed personas/demand; test the claims (S1).

These organize questions, not Phase 2 synthesis or automatic new features.

## Guides and safeguards

Interview: introduction, approved consent process, neutral prompts about a recent real task, follow-ups, close. Ask what happened; avoid selling the solution.
Usability: relevant goal without naming the control/path, observe actions, neutral probes, record assistance and outcome separately.
Record independent completion before verification prompts; prompted recovery is assisted.
Use test data when real data cannot be handled safely; label simulation limits.
Consent, accessible materials, voluntary exit, secure/minimal data and approved retention are prerequisites for sessions.
Use participants' assistive technology/devices where appropriate; plan mobile, connection and environment constraints.
Failed setup, withdrawal, unavailable artifacts and assisted completion need explicit recording.

## Handoff and verification

A guide does not authorize outreach, recording, paid tools or collecting personal data.
Do not invent consent/legal text or privacy policy.
Builders receive scoped requirements, study limitations and tests, not participant identities.
No responsive UI contract is generated by a research plan; specify study device coverage.
Verify neutral questions/tasks, feasible access, prerequisites, stop condition and no invented findings.
