# Copy

## Say the situation

Buttons and headings name the job. "Next: Waivers" beats "Next". "Move 9 visits" beats "Submit".
Why: the label is the decision.
Good: "Send 12 invoices". "Cap at $150/day".
Bad: "OK", "Submit", "Click here".
Check: the primary button says what will happen.

## Sample stays sample

Prices, quotes, reviews, and menus the owner did not supply are Sample, with a visible label.
Why: a rich screen that invents facts fails the honesty check.
Good: "Sample menu. Replace with your items and prices."
Bad: "$4.50 croissant" written as if the bakery said it.
Check: every number that is not Supplied or Verified is tagged Sample or Assumed in the doc, and Sample content is labeled on screen.

## One word per job

Use the label table. The same action does not switch between Save, Update, and Done.
Check: consistency.mjs, or a manual pass, finds one word per action.
