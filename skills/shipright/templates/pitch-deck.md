# Pitch deck

Status: template

Opt-in only. Make this when the owner asks for a pitch, a deck, or investor material. Traction, revenue, users, team, and the ask stay blank unless the owner supplied them.

Replace `Status: template` with `Status: current` when this file holds the project, not the blank.

Each slide is one section. Speaker notes sit under the slide. Paste each section into any slides tool.

## Title

- One line for the product.
- Speaker notes: say the line, then stop.

## Problem

- The problem in the owner's words.
- Speaker notes: no new facts.

## Who

- Who has the problem. Mark each fact Supplied, Verified, or Assumed.
- Speaker notes: skip anyone the owner did not agree to name.

## Solution

- What the product does. Screenshot paths only if the files exist.
- Speaker notes: show the screen, do not narrate a metric.

## How it works

- The core loop in order.
- Speaker notes: one pass through the loop.

## Why now

- Why now, and why this team, only from supplied facts.
- Speaker notes: if a year or a count is not in the record, leave it out.

## Market

- [Add your real numbers]
- A figure is allowed only on a line that says Verified and names a source, or says Assumed.
- Speaker notes: never fill this from memory.

## Model

- [Add your real numbers]
- Pricing only if the owner supplied it. Otherwise leave the blank.
- Speaker notes: do not invent a price.

## Competition

- Named alternatives the owner or the research brief already listed, and the gap.
- Speaker notes: no market share unless it is Verified on that line.

## Ask

- [Add your real numbers]
- Traction, revenue, users, team, and the ask. Blank unless the owner supplied them.
- Speaker notes: read the blank out loud rather than guessing.
