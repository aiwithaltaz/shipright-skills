# Case study

Status: template

Built only from the record, the pain map, the test report, and screenshots. No new numbers. No client names unless the owner agreed in writing.

Replace `Status: template` with `Status: current` when this file holds the project, not the blank.

## Problem

One line. The job that is failing.

## Who

Who it is for. Mark each fact Supplied, Verified, or Assumed.

## Research

What research found. Mark each fact Verified or Assumed. No invented quotes.

## Idea

The idea that was chosen, and the two ideas that were not. If no concept round was run, say that. Do not invent the missing ideas.

## Decisions

Three to five decisions, each with the tradeoff. Only decisions that are in DECISIONS.md.

## Pains

For an existing app: the top pains, with before and after screenshot paths that exist. If there is no after shot, say so.

## System

The design system in one image, or a sentence that no system image was made.

## Process

Stages run, rounds, and what changed after feedback. Only what the record says.

## Results

Only measured results: tests passed, steps saved, load time, accessibility checks. If it was not measured, write "not measured". Never invent business results.

## Next

What is next, from HANDOFF.md.
