# Step 4: Look and fix

Be a fresh critic. Ignore the plan and your intentions; judge only what the screen shows.

1. Screenshot every screen at 1280 (desktop) and 390 (phone).
2. Put them next to your memory of the references in `DESIGN.md`. Ask: does it fit the type and the mood? Does it look like a real shipped product or a template? Can a first-time user do the main job? Change one choice: does every label update?
3. Write the top 3 problems, fix them, and screenshot again.
4. At most 3 rounds. Stop early when nothing serious is left.

Then reply: the result, why, the next step. See [reply.md](../templates/reply.md).
