# Step 1: Product (write PRODUCT.md once)

Write one file, `PRODUCT.md`, at the project root. Keep each part short. Every later step reads it. Update it only when a fact changes.

## Idea
One paragraph: what it is, who pays, the job it does every day.

## Core (4 lines, 15 words each at most)
- **User:** one real kind of person.
- **Job:** what they come to do.
- **Key message:** the one thing they must believe or see.
- **Success:** how we know it worked (an action or a number).

## Research
3 lines from [Step 0](00-research.md). Each fact is Verified (with a link) or Assumed. For a redesign, link `BASELINE.md` and copy its craft bar line. A longer form: [research-brief.md](../templates/research-brief.md).

## Users
The main user and one secondary user (often a customer and an operator). Their day, why they open the app, what makes them anxious.

## PRD
The 3 to 5 jobs that matter, what stays out, and what the first screen shows each user: the core item and a direct action. Numbers come second. See [scope.md](../templates/scope.md).

## Flows and states
Write this part with [Step 1b: UX](01b-ux.md).
Each job in a few taps from where the user is. For every screen: empty, loading, failed, long text, big numbers. Records are editable. List the screens: for a tracker, Today, Week and a manage screen (Habits) are required. Follow the [clarity rules](clarity.md). See [screen-spec.md](../templates/screen-spec.md).

## Data
Realistic sample data, like a real business at month 6. Label it as sample. The user can keep it or clear it (Start fresh).

## Color
Write it with [Step 2a](02a-color.md): adopted brand colors, or 3 to 5 competitor palettes, the proposed palette and why.
