# Step 1: Product (write PRODUCT.md once)

Write one file, `PRODUCT.md`, at the project root. Keep each part short. Every later step reads it. Update it only when a fact changes.

## Idea
One paragraph: what it is, who pays, the job it does every day.

## Research
3 real alternatives of the same type (doing nothing counts) and what people dislike about them. Each fact is Verified (with a link) or Assumed. A longer form: [research-brief.md](../templates/research-brief.md).

## Users
The main user and one secondary user (often a customer and an operator). Their day, why they open the app, what makes them anxious.

## PRD
The 3 to 5 jobs that matter, what stays out, and what the first screen shows each user: the core item and a direct action. Numbers come second. See [scope.md](../templates/scope.md).

## Flows and states
Each job in a few taps from where the user is. For every screen: empty, loading, failed, long text, big numbers. Records are editable. See [screen-spec.md](../templates/screen-spec.md).

## Data
Realistic sample data, like a real business at month 6. Label it as sample.
