# Landing page and dev tool references
Lead with the product working, then proof, then install. One accent color.

- **Linear (linear.app):** dark, precise type, product shots as the hero.
- **Resend (resend.com):** code-first hero, generous space, crisp mono details.
- **Raycast (raycast.com):** rich product visuals, strong section rhythm.
- **Vercel (vercel.com):** neutral surfaces, Geist type, clear CTAs.
- **Stripe (stripe.com):** layered diagrams that explain how it works.
