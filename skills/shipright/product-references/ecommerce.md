# Ecommerce references
Product and price first, real photos, a cart that always shows the total.

- **Allbirds (allbirds.com):** clean product grid, simple variant picking.
- **Aesop (aesop.com):** editorial product pages with a strong mood.
- **Everlane (everlane.com):** honest pricing details, calm layout.
- **Apple Store (apple.com/shop):** step-by-step configure with a live price.
- **Shopify Dawn theme:** solid baseline for cart and checkout patterns.
