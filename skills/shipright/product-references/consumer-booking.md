# Consumer booking references
Customer side: mood, photography, the service and its price on the first screen. Owner side: a calm Today list.

- **Aesop (aesop.com):** warm neutrals, serif-led type, product and room photography, very calm pacing.
- **Glossier (glossier.com):** friendly type, soft color, big tap targets, clear prices.
- **Fresha (fresha.com):** fast service, staff and time picker; copy the flow, not the look.
- **Mindbody / ClassPass booking pages:** time-slot grids that read well on the phone.
- **Boutique hotel sites (e.g. Ace Hotel, The Hoxton):** photography and voice that sell a mood.
- **Resy / OpenTable:** compact date and time picking.
