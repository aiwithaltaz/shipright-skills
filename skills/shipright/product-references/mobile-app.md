# Mobile app references
Design at 390 first. One main action in thumb reach. Today on the first screen. Bottom tab bar, big tap targets, motion that confirms a tap.

- **Streaks (streaksapp.com):** big round habit tiles, one tap to complete, bold color.
- **Things 3 (culturedcode.com/things):** calm type, Today first, gentle detail.
- **Duolingo (duolingo.com):** streaks, celebration, playful illustration.
- **Headspace (headspace.com):** warm illustration and a soft palette.
- **Copilot Money / Monzo:** clear money formatting and simple charts.
- **Apple Fitness rings:** progress at a glance.
