# Tactile: soft clay toy
For: friendly, playful, beginner or family products (kids, first habits, self-care, pets, language practice).
Feel: squishy buttons, candy colors, a toy you want to press.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 display (44 px), Nunito for UI. Rounded and chunky.
- Color: lilac base, ONE pink accent for actions, mint for done, a different candy color tab per habit.
- Shape: big radius (24 to 32 px). Buttons have a solid bottom shadow and press down 3 px on tap.
- Detail: done rows fill with a mint wash; soft pink glow in the top corner.
- Never: thin lines, black backgrounds, serif type, grey corporate cards.
