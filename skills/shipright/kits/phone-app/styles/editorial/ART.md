# Editorial: a well-set almanac
For: calm, reflective, adult products (reading, journaling, sleep, mindful habits, personal finance for grown-ups).
Feel: printed page, ruled lines, ink. Quiet and confident.
- Type: Fraunces display, big (48 px title, optical size 144). Instrument Sans for UI. Italic Fraunces for numbers and streaks.
- Color: warm paper, near-black ink, ONE deep green for actions and done. No terracotta, no copper.
- Shape: rows are ruled lines, not cards. Small radius. No shadows.
- Detail: thick rule under the title, done items struck through, round dot history.
- Never: rounded floating cards, gradients, emoji, bright colors.
