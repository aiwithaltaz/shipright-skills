# Light intake

## Rule

Read what the owner already gave you. Do not ask the same question twice.
Ask **0-5 questions total before starting**, across the whole task, not once per stage.
Use zero questions when the task is already clear. For a vague product, aim for three useful questions.
Each requested decision counts separately. Do not bundle two decisions into one question.
After intake, do the work. Ask again only when a new choice would change the result.

## Two front doors

| Request | Start |
| --- | --- |
| Idea to build ("build me X") | build-pack, then the stages in SKILL.md |
| Rough idea without a job | product-design: Frame product, then stage 1 |
| Existing app to review or improve | stage 0 Discover |
| Explicit screen correction | ui-ux-design: the focused change |
| Launch readiness | ship-check, stage 10 |
| Exact feedback with crops | feedback round |

## Depth

| Depth | Questions | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick fix | Usually 0 | The requested label, spacing, or state |
| Focused improvement | 0-3 | The affected screen or flow, and its checks |
| New surface | Up to 5 total | Job, states, layout, and handoff |
| New product | Usually 3, maximum 5 total | Outcome, scope, mechanism, and flows |

Inspect the evidence before you ask. When the owner asked for product or research work, you may read sources that change a decision. Do not run a broad market scan unless they asked. Skip research on a focused correction, and skip it if they declined it.
If you cannot research, continue with Unknown facts and labeled hypotheses.
Plans do not authorize recruitment, recording, payment or messages.
Participant-guide questions are deliverables, not founder intake.

## Decision cards

Number the cards. Each card includes:

- **Why it matters:** what changes if the answer changes.
- Two or three options, each with a concrete example, plus **Something else**.
- **My pick:** a grounded recommendation, or **No pick yet**.
- **You decide:** the owner delegates that named choice.
- **Let me decide:** the owner reserves it. You continue the work that does not depend on it.

Use [the operating contract](operating-contract.md) for the facts ladder and the four statuses.
An example is not a requirement. A skipped choice stays unresolved. Silence is not approval.

## First reply on a vague idea

If the frame is not answered and not delegated, the first reply is the decision cards only.
Ask who it serves, what this phase finishes, and what stays out.
A fourth or fifth question needs a real blocker. Do not start objects, screens, flows, documents, or a research report.
A request to build it is not a frame or permission to invent.
After answers or a delegation, or when they asked for a draft with Unknown facts, continue.
Reserved choices stay open. Continue the independent work. One pause for the frame. Do not stop after every file.

## Request traps

| Request | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Make it premium without a job | Ask the screen's job. |
| Like this brand | Name the property you will reuse. Do not copy their identity or features. |
| Features without an outcome | Name the job and the narrow version. |
| Just say ready | Apply the Deadline rule. |

Missing context blocks only the commitment that needs it. Keep going on the rest.
